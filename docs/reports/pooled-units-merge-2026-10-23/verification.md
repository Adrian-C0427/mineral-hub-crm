# Third pass — Olene Reagan and Williams Heirs, placed against roads, river and surveys

Our earlier "not verified" came from testing these two against RRC's lateral lines, which turn out to be the wrong yardstick here: RRC's own bottom-hole for Winn CR 1H disagrees with the W-1 by 3,400 ft, and the Williams JWW well is not in RRC's layer under that name. So they were tested against things that cannot move: OpenStreetMap road/railroad/river geometry and RRC's survey polygons.

**Olene Reagan Gas Unit No. 1 — VERIFIED.** The plat is in *Robertson* County (Marquez survey A-25, west of the Navasota River), which is why our Leon-only survey check could never confirm it. Against the package polygon: FM 937 runs through the unit for 3,506 ft (the plat draws ~3,700 ft); TX 7 clips the connecting strip for 557 ft and the TX 7 / FM 937 junction sits outside, in the unit's southern bay — both exactly as drawn; the unit is 100% inside RRC's A-25 polygon and 2,294 ft west of the river (Robertson side); the Winn CR 1H W-1 bottom hole is inside it. The 180°-flipped alternative (the classic registration ambiguity) fails: it puts TX 7 4,312 ft inside and the junction inside the unit, which the plat contradicts. Shape check: our own independent trace of the plat matches the package outline with IoU 0.96.

**Williams Heirs Deep Gas Unit — VERIFIED.** The railroad runs along its NE edge (nearest 17 ft, never inside), FM 1512 runs just inside the east side (2,160 ft inside, at 78% of the width from the west), FM 1469 crosses the south end (955 ft inside at 15% of the height), and the FM 1469 / FM 1512 junction sits 216 ft outside the east edge — all exactly as the plat draws them. The unit straddles surveys A-351 / A-163 as labelled, and the Williams JWW 1H W-1 penetration point and first take point are inside it (the lateral starts in this unit, which is why RRC's downstream geometry never reached it). Drawn area −5.4% vs called 86.303 matches our own trace of the same plat (−3.9%).
